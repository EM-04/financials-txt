## What changed
*Movements of 5% or more, or 10,000 or more, whichever is larger.*

- Revenue rose 24.0% to 332,890, with growth weighted to the second half: the final
  three months contributed 114,550, against 72,560 in the first three.
- Cost of sales rose 35.2%, faster than revenue.
- Gross margin fell 2.4 points to 70.7% (prior period 73.1%). Derived from revenue and
  gross profit above.
- Operating expenses rose 16.7% to 199,800.
- Profit before tax rose 42.3% to 35,635.
- Cash at period end rose 43.5% to 88,120.

## Outlook
*Forward-looking. Not a forecast and not a guarantee.*

- The order book at 30 September 2026 stood at 141,000, covering approximately five
  months of revenue at the current run rate.
- The strategic report identifies recruitment as the principal constraint on delivery
  capacity in the coming year.
- Hannah Norwood, Managing Director (strategic report, page 6): "Next year's measure of
  success is margin, not growth."

## Risks
- Client concentration: the three largest clients represented 46% of revenue (prior
  period 52%).
- Recruitment: delivery capacity is constrained by hiring in a competitive market.
- Margin: cost of sales has grown faster than revenue in each of the last two periods.
