# Examples

## [norwood-kite.txt](norwood-kite.txt)

Norwood & Kite Design Ltd is fictional. The company number, the hash, the managing
director and her quotes are invented, and the register link will not resolve.

The example includes the parts a real publisher will be tempted to leave out.

Three of the six entries under *What changed* are adverse: cost of sales grew faster
than revenue, the margin fell, and overheads rose. The margin decline appears again
under *Risks* because it has now happened two years running. A summary that led on
revenue up 24% and profit up 42% would be true and would not conform, because the stated
threshold also admits the adverse movements and SPEC §5.1 requires them.

The entries follow the order of the headline table (§5.8). Revenue comes first because
it is the first row.

The managing director's quote says the company won "more work this year than in any
year before". The file could not say that in its own voice (§5.8). As an attributed
quote with a page reference, it is her view and is read as one. Her forward-looking
quote sits under *Outlook* (§5.5).

The file contains no comments, so these notes live here. A published file may not carry
anything a person reading it would not see (§5.8).
